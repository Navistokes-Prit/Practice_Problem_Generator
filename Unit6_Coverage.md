# Unit 6 Coverage — Radical & Rational Functions

## Page behaviour
- No skill dropdown and no difficulty dropdown.
- Selecting a strand generates one **hard** question from every subcategory in that strand.
- Enrichment remains a separate extra-hard strand.
- Students type with a physical keyboard; a MathJax preview renders live beside the input.
- Each practice card has Check Answer, Hint, and Worked Solution.
- Quiz mode generates 10 hard questions from RF13/RF14.

## RF13 — Radical functions (9 randomized families)
1. Domain and range of a transformed radical
2. Transformations of the square-root function
3. Endpoint under a function transformation
4. Equation from an endpoint, domain direction, and a point
5. Solve a transformed radical equation
6. Domain of y = sqrt(f(x))
7. Invariant points of y=f(x) and y=sqrt(f(x))
8. Recover f(x) from y=sqrt(f(x))
9. Identify a radical equation from a generated graph

## RF14 — Rational functions (13 randomized families)
1. Domain and non-permissible values
2. x- and y-intercepts
3. Vertical and horizontal asymptotes
4. Point of discontinuity (hole)
5. Complete feature analysis from factored form
6. Build y=a/(x-h)+k from asymptotes and a point
7. Solve a transformed reciprocal equation
8. Domain and range of a transformed reciprocal
9. Determine where a graph crosses its horizontal asymptote
10. Rational functions with no real discontinuities
11. Identify a reciprocal equation from a generated graph
12. Coordinate/sum question involving a hole
13. Construct an equation from an x-intercept, vertical asymptote, and hole

## Enrichment — 6 randomized families
1. Oblique asymptotes
2. Rational function simplifying to a line with two holes
3. Domain of sqrt(f(x)) for a rational f
4. Integrated rational/radical analysis
5. Recover a quadratic from y=sqrt(f(x))
6. Construct a rational function from mixed features

## Source alignment
- Lessons 7.1/7.2: radical transformations, domain/range, square roots of functions, and invariant points.
- Lessons 7.3–7.5: rational domains, discontinuities, vertical/horizontal asymptotes, holes, and graph analysis.
- Graphing practice: full feature analysis of rational/radical graphs and paired f(x), sqrt(f(x)) sketches.
- Unit exam: transformed radicals, rational intercepts/domain, reciprocal equations, holes, equation construction, and graph interpretation.
- Hard V1: equation construction from asymptotes, radical equation from endpoint/point/domain, and integrated rational-to-radical analysis.
- Oblique-asymptote material is placed in Enrichment because it appears in the teacher materials but is outside the Alberta RF14 outcome note.

## LaTeX validation
The generator deliberately uses `String.raw` for TeX-bearing templates. A randomized audit checks:
- recognized TeX commands inside every generated math fragment;
- no malformed command concatenations such as `\sqrtx`;
- balanced braces in generated math;
- no control characters created by JavaScript string escapes;
- unique multiple-choice options.
